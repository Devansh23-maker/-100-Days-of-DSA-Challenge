📚 Topics Covered

- Search Space Optimization

- Scheduling & Allocation Problems

---

✅ Problems Solved

1. Square Root of a Number

Platform: LeetCode (LC 69)

Approach:

- Applied Binary Search on the range [1, x].
- Checked whether "mid × mid" was less than or equal to "x" and narrowed the search accordingly.

Time Complexity: O(log n)

Space Complexity: O(1)

---

2. Koko Eating Bananas

Platform: LeetCode (LC 875)

Approach:

- Binary searched the eating speed.
- Calculated the total hours required for each speed and selected the minimum feasible speed.

Time Complexity: O(n log m) (m = maximum pile size)

Space Complexity: O(1)

---

3. Monkey and the Oiled Bamboo

Platform: LightOJ 1425

Approach:

- Binary searched the minimum jump strength.
- Simulated Monkey's jumps for every candidate value to verify feasibility.

Time Complexity: O(n log m)

Space Complexity: O(1)

---

🧠 Key Learnings

- Binary Search can be performed on the answer instead of the data.
- The search space should be monotonic—if one answer works, all larger answers (or smaller answers) should also work.
- Simulation functions are commonly used to validate each candidate answer.
- Binary Search on Answer is one of the highest-frequency interview patterns.
