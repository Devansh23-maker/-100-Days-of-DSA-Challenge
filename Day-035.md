✅ Problems Solved
1. Reverse Linked List

Platform: LeetCode — LC 206

Approach:

Used three pointers: prev, curr, and next.
Reversed each node's next pointer while traversing the list.

Time Complexity: O(n)
Space Complexity: O(1)

2. Middle of the Linked List

Platform: LeetCode — LC 876

Approach:

Used the Fast & Slow Pointer technique.
slow moves one step while fast moves two steps.
When fast reaches the end, slow is at the middle.

Time Complexity: O(n)
Space Complexity: O(1)

🧠 Key Learnings
prev → curr → next is the core pattern for reversing a Linked List.
Fast & Slow pointers are extremely important for Linked List problems.
Both problems can be solved with O(1) extra space.
Drawing the pointers on paper makes Linked List problems much easier to understand.
