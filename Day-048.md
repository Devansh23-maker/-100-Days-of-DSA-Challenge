🧠 Key Learnings
Bit Manipulation works directly with the binary representation of numbers.
& is useful for checking and masking bits.
| is useful for setting bits.
^ is especially useful for finding unique elements.
<< generally multiplies a number by 2.
>> generally divides a positive number by 2.
n & 1 can determine whether a number is odd or even.
Bit manipulation can provide very efficient solutions with O(1) extra space.
