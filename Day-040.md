2. Intersection of Two Linked Lists

Platform: LeetCode — LC 160

Approach:

Use two pointers, p1 and p2.
When a pointer reaches the end of its list, move it to the head of the other list.
Both pointers will eventually meet at the intersection node.

Time Complexity: O(n + m)
Space Complexity: O(1)

🧠 Key Learnings
Two pointers can solve Linked List intersection without extra memory.
Switching each pointer to the other list equalizes the distance traveled.
Don't confuse intersection by value with intersection by node — the actual node/reference must be the same.
Array revision helps maintain previously learned patterns while progressing through Linked Lists.
